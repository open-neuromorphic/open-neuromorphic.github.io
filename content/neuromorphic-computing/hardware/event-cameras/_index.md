Event cameras are bio-inspired vision sensors that operate asynchronously, mimicking the transient response of biological retinal ganglion cells. Instead of capturing standard frames at fixed clock intervals, each autonomous pixel detects local changes in illumination and transmits timestamped spike events with microsecond temporal resolution.
