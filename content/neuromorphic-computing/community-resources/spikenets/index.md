[SpikeNets](https://spikenets.substack.com/) is a bi-weekly newsletter dedicated to neuromorphic artificial intelligence, spiking neural networks (SNNs), and bio-inspired machine intelligence.

Published on Substack, each issue provides accessible yet technically grounded deep dives into cutting-edge research, hands-on implementations, and emerging tools across the brain-inspired computing landscape.

## Focus Areas

- **Paper Discussions & Research Summaries:** In-depth analyses of recent publications, theoretical models, and foundational literature across computational neuroscience and neuromorphic engineering.
- **Experiments & Benchmarks:** Practical implementations, code walkthroughs, and investigations into biologically plausible learning algorithms, evolution strategies, and event-driven computing.
- **Tools & Ecosystem:** Highlights and reviews of open-source frameworks, neuromorphic hardware architectures, and developer tooling.
- **Perspectives & Commentary:** Critical commentary on the evolution of NeuroAI, hardware-software co-design, and moving beyond conventional deep learning paradigms.
